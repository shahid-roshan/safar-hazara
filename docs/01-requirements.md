# Safar Hazara: Requirements

## Problem
1. Mashhoor jagahon ke baare mein sab jaante hain (Facebook aur videos ki wajah se), lekin kam mashhoor jagahon ki sahi info aur detail nahi milti.
2. Tourist ko trip ka poora plan khud research karke banana padta hai: kahan rukna hai, kitna waqt lagega, kaun sa hotel.
3. Hotels ke rate aur services alag hote hain; sahi pata karne ke liye khud ja kar reception par poochna padta hai.
4. Local guide sab kuch jaanta hai, lekin woh paise leta hai, aur har tourist ke liye mumkin nahi.
5. Tayyari bhi khud karni padti hai: kaun se kapre (garam), kaun si gaari (jaise 4x4 jeep), aur hiking wagaira ke liye kya chahiye.

## Users
1. **Tourist**: Wo banda jo Hazara ghoomne aata hai. Jagahein aur hotels dekhta hai, map khol kar dekhta hai, apna trip banata hai.
2. **Admin**: Website chalane wala. Jagahein aur hotels add karta hai, aur reviews ko theek ya ghalat dekh kar approve karta hai.

## Goal
Safar Hazara ka maqsad tourist ko ek hi jagah par Hazara ki jagahon, hotels aur trip planning ki info dena hai, taake usay sab kuch khud research na karna pade.
